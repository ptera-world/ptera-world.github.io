a map of programs i like, programs i don't, and why. none of it's provable. that's the point.

## what is this?

everywhere else on this site tries to argue something - a mechanism, a pattern, a structural claim you could push back on with evidence. this collection doesn't. it's just taste: the software that feels right to use, the software that doesn't, and the reasons, such as they are.

subjective isn't the same as arbitrary. there are usually reasons. they just don't generalize to "you should agree."

## how do i navigate?

- scroll to zoom
- click a node for its card, click again for the full panel
- drag to pan
- arrow keys to move between nodes
- `/` to search
